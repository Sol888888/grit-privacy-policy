1. Introduction

Welcome to GRIT ("we," "our," or "us"). GRIT is operated by Magpie Technology Limited, a company based in Hong Kong. GRIT is a life-RPG platform designed to help men build brotherhood, stay accountable, and improve their lives through missions, habits, and supportive communities.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the GRIT mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

When you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, login method, profile photo
  • Profile Data: Lifestyle interests, goals, age range, city/region
  • User-Generated Content: Photos you upload for missions, check-ins, and personal progress
  • In-App Activity: Missions completed, connection activity, membership, progression milestones
  • Support Requests: Messages or attachments you send to customer support

We do not collect credit card numbers. All purchases happen through the App Store or Google Play.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

We may collect limited technical data such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Country/region (approximate, based on device settings)
  • Basic app performance metrics
  • Error logs and crash diagnostics

We do not track you across other apps or websites.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

Google Login (Google Identity Services)

If you sign in with Google, you may grant permission for us to receive:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture

We do not receive any other Google data (contacts, files, location, calendars, etc.).

Sign in with Apple

Apple may provide:

  • Your name (if you allow)
  • Email address (or Apple Private Relay email)

We do not share any information back to Apple beyond authentication.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Service: Create your account, manage your profile, enable tribe interactions, track mission progress
  • Personalize Your Experience: Suggest challenges, content, or tribes relevant to your goals
  • Process In-App Purchases: Through Apple, Google, and RevenueCat
  • Enhance Safety: Assist in preventing fraud or misuse of the platform
  • Improve the App: Analyze performance and improve features
  • Communicate With You: Notifications, mission reminders, account alerts, support responses

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only when necessary:

4.1 With Other Users

  • Your publicly visible profile (name, photo, goals)
  • Content you choose to upload to missions or tribes
  • Messages you send inside group or private chats

4.2 With Service Providers

We use trusted vendors to help us operate the App:

  • Firebase/Google Cloud - login authentication, storage
  • Google Analytics Reporting Tools – for performance and diagnostics

These providers are contractually required to protect your data.

4.3 Legal Compliance

We may disclose information when required by law, regulation, or legal process.

4.4 Business Transfers

If Magpie Technology Limited is acquired or merged, your data may transfer as part of the business.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

  • Account Data: Deleted within 90 days after you delete your account
  • Uploaded Photos: Deleted along with your account
  • Chat Messages: Deleted when your account is deleted
  • Analytics/Diagnostic Data: May be kept in anonymized form

You can request deletion at any time by emailing support@thegritapp.com.
